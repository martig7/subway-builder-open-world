- **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, delivered through [Geofabrik](https://www.geofabrik.de/data/download.html). Roads, buildings, land use, water, place names, and road/ferry routing inputs. [OpenStreetMap attribution and ODbL license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- **U.S. Census Bureau — [LEHD LODES8, 2023, JT01](https://lehd.ces.census.gov/data/lodes/LODES8/)**. Employment OD data and crosswalks used to model NEC/New York commuter demand; processed by Open World. These are employment statistics, not observed transit trips.

- **Statistics Bureau of Japan / [e-Stat](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/)** — 2020 Population Census and 2021 Economic Census. Commuting/school OD tables, population and employment meshes, and statistical boundaries used for Japan demand and prefecture ownership; processed by Open World. [Terms of use](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/terms-of-use).
- **Geospatial Information Authority of Japan — 国土地理院最適化ベクトルタイル**, with **© OpenStreetMap contributors** where recorded. [Optimal Vector Tiles](https://cyberjapandata.gsi.go.jp/xyz/optimal_bvmap-v1/optimal_bvmap-v1.pmtiles) supplement building footprints/anchors in selected Japanese coverage gaps. [GSI source and usage conditions](https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html).
- **[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)**. Land, lakes, and country boundaries for the worldwide background map. “Made with Natural Earth.” [Public-domain terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
- **NASA MODIS MCD12Q1 v061 (2023), supplied through NASA GIBS / ESDIS**. [Annual land-cover classification](https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/layer-metadata/v1.0/MODIS_Combined_L3_IGBP_Land_Cover_Type_Annual.json) resampled and simplified by Open World into a worldwide vegetation overview: forests, shrubland, savanna, grassland, and wetlands. This is land cover, not a park inventory. [NASA data-use guidance](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/data-use-policy).

Detailed input URLs and available checksums are retained in the World source
locks and map-creator provenance records. Software license notices are separate
from these dataset attributions.

## Japan weekday non-commute movements

- **Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) — 2015 Sixth National Survey of Net Passenger Flow (全国幹線旅客純流動調査).** The [weekday actual origin-to-destination, representative-mode, 207-life-area workbook](https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001297377.xls) (`2015DW_207OD_DK.xls`) supplies national trunk-travel counts. We use the all-purpose/all-mode sheet once, retain directed movements, and exclude same-prefecture cells. The survey excludes commuting and schooling and omits travel internal to three metropolitan groups. [MLIT table index](https://www.mlit.go.jp/sogoseisaku/soukou/sogoseisaku_soukou_fr_000018.html) and [survey definitions](https://www.mlit.go.jp/statistics/details/t-ryokaku-2_tk_000008.html).
- **Tokyo Metropolitan Area Transportation Planning Council — 2018 Tokyo Person Trip survey.** The [purpose × representative-mode extract](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000032066127&fileKind=1) and [zone-code workbook](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/static/hp/file/data/H30_zonecode.xlsx) supply the Tokyo/Saitama/Chiba/Kanagawa cross-prefecture replacement. [Provider's 2018 data page](https://www.tokyo-pt.jp/data/H30). Coverage is surveyed residents and excludes Tokyo island municipalities.
- **Chukyo Metropolitan Area Transportation Planning Council / MLIT Chubu Regional Development Bureau — 2022 Chukyo Person Trip survey, final results.** The [purpose × representative-mode extract](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000040268715&fileKind=0) and [zone-code workbook](https://www.cbr.mlit.go.jp/kikaku/chukyo-pt/offer/pdf/20260622_code.xlsx) supply the Aichi/Gifu/Mie cross-prefecture replacement. [Provider's data page](https://www.cbr.mlit.go.jp/kikaku/chukyo-pt/offer/index.html). Gifu and Mie coverage is limited to surveyed portions.
- **Keihanshin Metropolitan Area Transportation Planning Council / MLIT Kinki Regional Development Bureau — 2021 Kinki Person Trip survey, final results.** The [purpose × representative-mode extract](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000040170629&fileKind=0) supplies the Osaka/Kyoto/Hyogo/Nara cross-prefecture replacement. [Provider's data page](https://www.kkr.mlit.go.jp/plan/pt/data/index.html).
- **MLIT — [2005 municipality correspondence for the 207 life-area zones](https://www.mlit.go.jp/seisakutokatsu/jyunryuudou/doc/207_Zone2005.pdf).** Used to identify national survey zones and derive representative map points with the already-attributed e-Stat boundaries; the game currently aggregates the national rows to prefecture controls before endpoint sampling.

The regional non-commute estimates include explicitly business/private movements plus a **project-modeled fraction** of unspecified return-home movements, `q = B / (B + C)` for each survey region. They are not a published official non-commute total. The resulting [movement ledger and sampling method](worlds/japan/data/noncommute/README.md) represent one-way movements on a representative weekday, not unique travelers or an extra return trip. Exact input filenames, byte counts, and SHA-256 hashes are in the [source lock](worlds/japan/data/noncommute/sources.lock.json). The separate 2024 foreign-visitor comparison is **not** included in the installed movement total.

## High-speed rail and maglev

- **Deutsche Bahn / Siemens — ICE 3neo**. [Operator specifications](https://www.bahn.de/service/ueber-uns/zugtypen/ice-3neo): 320 km/h, eight cars, 201 m and 439 seats. Homogeneous game cars average 25.125 m and round to 55 seats. [2020 procurement announcement](https://assets.new.siemens.com/siemens/assets/api/uuid:cfaa1a08-e7b2-446d-b046-0cc18dc6994f/DB-invests-one-billion-euros-in-new-ICE.pdf): approximately EUR 1 billion for 30 sets, allocated to USD 4.75 million/car using the [Federal Reserve's 2020 annual exchange rate](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g5a/20210104/).
- **US Federal Railroad Administration — [Maglev Deployment Program, September 2005](https://railroads.dot.gov/sites/fra.dot.gov/files/fra_net/1176/maglev-sep05.pdf)**. Transrapid section dimensions, capacity, formations and acceleration support rounded inputs of 25 m, 3.7 m, 100 seats, 3–10 cars and 1 m/s². Baltimore's USD 11.7 million/car planning estimate (2002 dollars) becomes approximately USD 16.8 million in 2020 dollars using [BLS CPI-U](https://www.bls.gov/cpi/tables/supplemental-files/historical-cpi-u-202108.pdf). This is a planning proxy, not a Shanghai procurement quote.
- **China National Railway Administration — [Shanghai maglev speed history](https://www.nra.gov.cn/xwzx/xwxx/xwlb/202204/t20220405_280276.shtml)**. The game's 430 km/h is historically operated capability. [Shanghai's current guide](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Transportation/20240102/44f499a17b324b25996f2d58fcbf5f23.html) advertises 300 km/h service.
- **World Bank — [China's High-Speed Rail Development (2019)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933411559841476316/pdf/Chinas-High-Speed-Rail-Development.pdf)** and the FRA report provide infrastructure cost context. Platform margins, operating-cost splits, infrastructure unit costs and unsupported physics values are explicitly labeled game assumptions in the [full train research and calculation record](docs/research/high-speed-maglev-trains.md).
- **Subway Builder — [Train Types API](https://www.subwaybuilder.com/docs/api-reference/trains)**. Registration fields and units used by [the shared train definitions](open-world-platform/src/runtime/intercity-trains.js). The native game owns purchases, inventory, route assignment, simulation and saves.
