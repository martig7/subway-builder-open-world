Kansas City tile handoff

This throwaway logic prototype asks whether two independently saved Subway Builder tiles can share one authoritative clock, wallet, and commuter ledger while transitions remain atomic, retryable, and mass-conserving.

Prototype only. Everything is in memory. No game, files, or database are touched. The transition reducer is isolated from the page so its state decisions can be compared with the real runtime.
