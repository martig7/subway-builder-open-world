# Full-prefecture Gifu–Mie–Aichi OD sources

Date: 2026-09-11. Scope: official and first-party sources for completing the portions of the six directed Gifu–Mie–Aichi flows that lie outside the 2022 Chukyo Person Trip (PT) survey area.

## Conclusion

There is no free official table that supplies a complete, purpose-specific, all-mode weekday OD for all residents of the three prefectures. The practical way to observe the remaining geography without imposing a road or public-transport mode share is a **licensed mobile-network OD product**. NTT DOCOMO's Mobile Spatial Statistics population-flow product is the clearest fit. It estimates daily origin-to-destination movements nationwide at prefecture, municipality, or approximately 1 km resolution and can also classify residence area. The published specification covers ages 15–79 and periods from October 2015 onward. [Population-flow product](https://mobaku.jp/service/jpn_flow/od/), [method and population expansion](https://mobaku.jp/about/)

Use the mobile data as a **coverage-uplift control**, not as a replacement for PT purpose data. Request total-person OD with no mode filter, split only by whether the traveller's inferred residence municipality is inside or outside the Chukyo PT survey area. For each directed prefecture pair, scale the PT non-commute total by the ratio

`mobile all-resident pair flow / mobile survey-area-resident pair flow`.

This retains PT's purpose classification while using a mode-neutral observation to add the movements of residents whom PT did not sample. Pool ordinary non-holiday weekdays over several months because low-volume strata can be suppressed. Obtain explicit rights to retain and distribute derived game controls.

## What the Chukyo PT survey already contains

The coverage gap is smaller than the survey map alone suggests. The survey sampled residents of 97 municipalities: all Aichi, 17 cities and 16 towns in southern Gifu, and 5 cities and 5 towns in northern Mie. Its population expansion estimates movement only for residents of that area. [Official survey scope and expansion method, pp. 2–3](https://www.cbr.mlit.go.jp/kikaku/chukyo-pt/persontrip/pdf/no06_honpen.pdf), [official coverage summary](https://ptplatform.mlit.go.jp/uploads/action/chukyo_toshiken_pt.pdf)

However, the published purpose OD records origins and destinations outside the survey area when a sampled resident travelled there. The workbook contains the special aggregate endpoints `岐阜県_圏域外` and `三重県_圏域外`, alongside the corresponding prefecture `_圏域内` summaries. These are distinct from zone-workbook codes `29999`=`岐阜県` and `39999`=`三重県`, which are whole-prefecture aggregate labels, not names for the uncovered remainder. [Purpose OD workbook](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?fileKind=0&statInfId=000040268809), [zone-code workbook](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?fileKind=0&statInfId=000040269413)

Therefore:

- keep rows involving `岐阜県_圏域外` and `三重県_圏域外`; they are observed all-mode pieces of the missing geography generated by sampled residents;
- do not interpret them as full-prefecture totals, because residents of northern Gifu and central/southern Mie were not sampled;
- the custom aggregation system and restricted master data cannot create those missing residents. Finer access changes the aggregation, not the survey population.

## Recommended acquisition and merge

1. Preserve the 2022 PT rows for every covered zone and the `岐阜県_圏域外`/`三重県_圏域外` endpoints. Apply the existing commute/school removal and return-home attribution before computing the non-commute base.
2. Ask a mobile-data vendor for ordinary non-holiday weekday OD among full Gifu, Mie, and Aichi, with traveller residence classified into the 97 PT municipalities versus the remainder. Request total persons only; do not condition the order on mode.
3. Calculate a separate coverage-uplift ratio for each of the six directions. Compare a 2019 pre-pandemic period with an October–November 2022 period and use the difference as uncertainty rather than silently mixing vintages.
4. Apply the ratios to PT non-commute mass and multiply the resulting weekday control by 365. Keep the direct PT external-zone contribution, mobile uplift, period, age limitation, suppression, and uncertainty separately auditable.
5. If licensed mobile data is unavailable, retain the current mode-neutral extrapolation for uncovered residents. No public official source found in this review removes that modeling step.

An alternative hybrid is useful if the vendor cannot provide traveller-residence strata. Let `M_full` be full-prefecture mobile OD, `M_cov` the portion reproduced for the covered PT geography, and let `C_full` and `C_cov` be the corresponding all-mode Population Census commute/school OD matrices. Fit a scaling factor `α` on the covered geography so that `M_cov - αC_cov` reproduces PT non-commute OD, then estimate the uncovered contribution as `(M_full - M_cov) - α(C_full - C_cov)`, constrained to be non-negative. This uses the Census only to remove commute/school—not to supply a transport mode—and calibration is essential because Census cells are usual worker/student flows rather than observed daily trips. Treat disagreement between this subtraction and the simpler mobile coverage-uplift result as model uncertainty. [2020 Census prefecture commute/school OD table](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0003454526)

This approach completes the last six flows without importing observed road, rail, or bus shares into the game. It does add a commercial-data dependency and a modeled purpose assumption for the uncovered residents; it is not a new public e-Stat table.
